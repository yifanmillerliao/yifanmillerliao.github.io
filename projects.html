Which Chess Games Get Engine Analysis?
A selection-bias audit of 14.3 million Lichess games
Selection Bias
Inverse Probability Weighting
Clustered Data
Online Chess

Lichess publishes millions of rated games each month, but engine evaluations appear only when a user requests analysis. In November 2017, just 7.5% of games had them. This project asks whether that convenient subset represents the full population, and how much adjustment can reduce the difference.

Rechecking a Chromosome 7 CRISPR Screen
Does the deleted-region enrichment hold when neighboring genes are not treated as independent?
Genomics
Spatial Dependence
Permutation Tests
R

I found that neighboring genes on chromosome 7 are mildly correlated, but the central finding of a 2022 CRISPR screen, that genes inside commonly deleted regions show stronger tumor-suppressor activity, still holds when this local correlation is taken into account.
